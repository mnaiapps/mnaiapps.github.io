Welcome to ChatExpense

Architecture, navigation hierarchy, and privacy foundation
Getting Started

ChatExpense combines double-entry accounting integrity with an intelligent on-device Natural Language Understanding engine. All data is persisted in private local SQLite databases on your Apple device. When CloudKit synchronization is enabled, records synchronize across your devices with end-to-end encryption—no central server ever sees or stores your financial transactions.

Universal Quick Action '+'

Workspaces & Shared Books

Separate personal finances from family budgets and shared projects
Getting Started

Workspaces act as independent accounting vaults. You have a private Personal Workspace by default, and you can create or join CloudKit-shared Workspaces to manage expenses cooperatively with family members, partners, or project collaborators.

1
Switch Active Workspace
Tap your active workspace badge in Settings or the top navigation bar to open the Workspace Switcher.
2
Create a Shared Workspace
Tap 'New Workspace', specify the book name, and send an Apple CloudKit invite link via Messages, Mail, or AirDrop.
3
Strict Isolation Firewall
Every transaction, account, budget, and bill is strictly scoped to its active workspace. Cross-workspace data leaks are architecturally impossible.
💡 Pro Tips
  • Workspace participants can see and add shared records, but cannot inspect your Personal Workspace.
  • Each workspace can maintain its own base reporting currency (e.g., USD for Personal, EUR for a European trip workspace).

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding privacy, sync, and troubleshooting
Support
Q
Is any of my financial data uploaded to external AI servers?
No. The conversational parser, budget projections, receipt OCR, and financial analytics all execute 100% locally on your device. We do not use third-party cloud AI APIs.
Q
How does iCloud sync work across multiple devices?
When enabled, ChatExpense uses Apple's private CloudKit database linked exclusively to your personal Apple ID. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple.
Q
Can I restore data if I change my iPhone?
Yes. By enabling iCloud Sync in Settings, signing into the same Apple ID on your new device will automatically download your workspaces, accounts, transactions, and attachments.
Q
Can I use the app completely offline without an internet connection?
Yes. ChatExpense is offline-first. Transactions, receipt scans, calculations, and AI commands function perfectly with zero network connectivity.
